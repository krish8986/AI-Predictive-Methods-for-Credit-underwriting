# RBI Guidelines Reference

## Customer Communication

Lenders should communicate material terms, fees, interest rates, repayment schedules, and
credit decisions clearly to customers. This reference document is informational and must be
reviewed against current official Reserve Bank of India publications before use in production.

## Fair Lending

Credit assessments should follow documented, consistent policies. Decisions should not rely on
protected characteristics except where required by applicable law or regulation.

## Data Handling

Customer data should be collected, processed, retained, and disclosed in accordance with
applicable law, internal controls, and approved security practices.
