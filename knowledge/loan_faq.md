# Loan FAQ

## What affects a loan decision?

Income, active loans, credit history, requested amount, loan term, interest rate, assets, and
application completeness can affect an underwriting decision.

## What is a credit score?

A credit score is one indicator of repayment history and credit behavior. It is evaluated with
other information rather than used as the only decision factor.

## Can a declined application be reviewed?

Applicants can contact the lender to understand available next steps and whether a manual review
or a future application is appropriate.
