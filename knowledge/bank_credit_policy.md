# Bank Credit Policy

## Assessment Principles

Credit decisions should consider repayment capacity, existing obligations, credit history,
loan purpose, and declared assets. Applicants must provide accurate and complete information.

## Decision Outcomes

An application may be approved, declined, or referred for manual review. A model result is an
input to underwriting and does not replace required policy, compliance, or human-review checks.

## Documentation

Identity, address, income, and asset information may require verification before disbursement.
Additional documents can be requested when application data is incomplete or inconsistent.
